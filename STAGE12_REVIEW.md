## Failed Budgets

### Linux list_high

The candidate exceeded four practical relative budgets under negligible A/A
calibration noise:

| Metric | Baseline | Candidate | Regression | Budget |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| p50 latency | 8.675 ms | 25.135 ms | 189.742% | 100% |
| p95 latency | 10.803 ms | 27.285 ms | 152.574% | 125% |
| p99 latency | 10.873 ms | 29.366 ms | 170.073% | 150% |
| p50 CPU | 8.031 ms | 24.653 ms | 206.973% | 200% |

The baseline calibration deltas were 0.043% at p50, 0.022% at p95, 0.053% at
p99, and 0.025% for p50 CPU. Noise therefore does not explain the threshold
crossings. Both artifacts returned exactly 1,024 rows in all 10,000 comparison
samples with no failures.

Linux also classified `startup_list_warm` and `snapshot_empty` as INCONCLUSIVE
because their same-artifact calibration exceeded the declared metric-specific
noise limits. Those caveats do not weaken or hide the absolute Linux FAIL.

### Windows why_full_matrix

The complete Why matrix had zero failures and a 32 ms p99, but one retained
invocation reached 4.625 seconds. This exceeds the predeclared 1-second absolute
maximum, so the workload and platform verdict are FAIL regardless of
calibration. `list_typical` was separately INCONCLUSIVE because maximum-latency
calibration noise exceeded its declared limit.

## Protocol Corrections

Qualification uncovered and corrected infrastructure defects without weakening
the benchmark:

- Windows MSVC helper executables were not byte-reproducible across clean builds
  with unchanged source, lockfile, Rust toolchain, and runner image. The final
  protocol verifies frozen source, lockfile, Rust version, target, behavior, and
  records the actual helper hash in the manifest and every helper-executed row.
- The controller now has a five-hour run bound within a six-hour workflow job;
  sample counts and workload shapes are unchanged.
- Windows replacement QA now uses a distinct owner process, matching the product
  replacement contract instead of depending on incidental partial ownership.
- Manifest duration validation now derives its ceiling from the frozen plan.

Smoke runs `30072027107` and `30072549885` passed all QA and benchmark jobs.
Their summaries were independently regenerated byte-for-byte before full
collection.
